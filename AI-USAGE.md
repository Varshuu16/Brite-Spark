# AI Usage Disclosure

AI was used as a learning and development assistance tool while building the BriteSpark Policy Assistant.

It was used for research into unfamiliar topics, exploring possible approaches, evaluating implementation options, debugging, test case suggestions, improving documentation and comments, and design feedback for the user interface and its features.

The suggestions were reviewed while building the system, and were modified when appropriate to meet the requirements for the task and the implementation details.

The system was tested with automated unit tests, adversarial testing, the evaluation dataset, and manual testing, with a focus on policy grounding, citations, temporal logic, refusal, and policy conflict resolution.

The source policy documents were retained to ensure that policy content was not modified.
